# Objection handling

Load for: sales calls, FAQ sections, support replies, community answers. Distills: S2 open questions and QA issues, the claim ledger, and the battlecards. Where the honest answer is "not yet", it says not yet. Add new objections from `ops/signal-log.md` when a WATCH reaches its threshold.

## "Where does your data come from?"
Honest answer today: "RoomIQ is built from observed room listings in the markets it covers, tracked over time at the room level. Every metric shows its as-of date, and every property shows an Evidence Quality score so you can see how complete the history is."
Never name or imply a source, a partner, or a collection method (D-04). If pushed: "I'll get you a written answer on sourcing" and log the signal as OBJECTION.

## "How accurate is the forecast?"
"We don't claim accuracy yet. What you get instead is the forecast's working: the formula, the number of comps, how far away they are, whether local evidence was enough, and a confidence label. On a thin-evidence address it will tell you that, in red."
If the founder publishes a backtest (G14), this answer changes by AMEND.

## "Does it cover my market?"
"Run your address and RoomIQ shows nearby coliving supply within about three miles first. If evidence is thin, the forecast says fallback support and shows its confidence." In a live call, choose the address yourself only in covered metros (D-06).

## "My house isn't on any platform yet. Does that matter?"
"No. The Property Predictor underwrites any US address from your baseline profile: type, bedrooms, bathrooms, room mix, and if nearby evidence is thin the forecast says fallback support. Tracked properties use their own history; new ones use the bounded underwriting path."

## "Why not just use AirDNA?"
"AirDNA is built for nightly stays. A rent-by-the-room house earns weekly, room by room, and its rooms earn differently by bathroom and size. RoomIQ models that unit."

## "I already have a spreadsheet."
"Keep it. Put RoomIQ's forecast next to your room rent assumption and see where they differ, with the comps behind the difference."

## "Are the AI images real?"
"No, and the product says so on every one: AI-generated, not actual property condition, watermarked into the downloaded file. They are concepts for planning, with no validated rent impact yet."

## "Can I give this to my lender?"
"You can share the underwrite as your own analysis. RoomIQ isn't a lender product or an appraisal, and the Deal Calculator is for information, not financial advice." Do not say "lender-ready".

## "What does it cost?"
Pricing is not decided (D-11). Say: "I'll send current plans after the call." Never quote a number.

## "Is rent-by-the-room legal where I'm buying?"
"RoomIQ compares an operating checklist with recorded local policies, but it isn't legal advice. Check local occupancy and rooming-house rules with someone qualified." House rules output is UNVERIFIED (C24): do not describe it.

## "What does Market Score measure?"
"It's a 0 to 100 score with a letter grade that ranks markets for this model." Do not list inputs until G18 is answered.
