# Value proposition, acquiring investors

Load for: any asset whose audience is Audience A. Never mix with `value-proposition-operators.md` in one asset. Distills: master context Section 10. Status: WORKING (D-07).

## Brand promise
Know what each room can earn before you buy the house, and how much to trust the number.

## Positioning for this audience
You already know rent-by-the-room economics live or die on the room. RoomIQ gives you room-level evidence for any US address, and where nearby evidence is thin it labels the forecast fallback support with its confidence, so a deal decision rests on observed rents instead of a guess in a spreadsheet.

## Pillar 1, find the market
**Headline options:** "Start where rooms fill." / "See which US markets rank highest before you look at a single house."
**Reasons to believe (SHIPPED or PARTIAL only):**
- Choose up to 3 US cities, ZIPs, or a metro group per dashboard (C01).
- Top US markets ranked by a 0 to 100 Market Score with a letter grade (C02, PARTIAL: name it, do not explain inputs).
- Seasonality of occupancy and fill speed, with the national pattern labeled when local data is thin (C03).
- A submarket map that bands cells Strong, Watch, Weak, or Thin, evidence-adjusted (C05).

## Pillar 2, underwrite at the room level
**Headline options:** "Underwrite the room, not just the house." / "A forecast that shows its work."
**Reasons to believe:**
- Underwrite any US address. Where nearby evidence is thin, RoomIQ labels the forecast fallback support and shows its confidence (C27).
- The formula prints under the number: price times rooms times occupancy times 4.35 weeks (C27).
- See nearby coliving supply within about 3 miles before you run anything (C21).
- Test the forecast against a shorter or longer evidence window (C28).
- Bathroom mix and room mix are first-class inputs, because private-bath supply drives revenue (FACT, baseline profile copy).

## Pillar 3, decide with the financing math
**Headline options:** "Carry the forecast into the deal." / "From room revenue to DSCR in one flow."
**Reasons to believe (PARTIAL, describe inputs only):**
- On RoomIQ Pro, the Deal Calculator prefills monthly room revenue from the forecast and takes your purchase and financing inputs, for cash flow, CoC, DSCR, and cap rate (C31). For information, not financial advice.
- Saved underwrites appear in My Underwrites for re-running later (C32).

## What we never promise this audience
Accuracy, returns, a "good deal", lender acceptance, legal approval of a conversion, or data from any named source.
