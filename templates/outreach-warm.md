# Template, warm outreach

For people who asked, engaged, or were introduced. Not for cold lists: outreach at volume is out of scope (D-10).
Audience: [A / B]. Relationship: [asked in a thread / introduced by / met at]. Purpose: [design-partner invite / follow-up after a demo].

## Structure
- **Subject or first line.** Their context, not ours: "Your question about the garage conversion in [city]".
- **Why you are writing.** One sentence tied to what they said or did.
- **The offer.** One concrete thing: "I can run that address through RoomIQ with you in 30 minutes and show you the room-level forecast, including how confident it is."
- **The honest limit.** "If the address is in a market with thin evidence, it will say so."
- **One ask.** A time, or a yes or no.

## Rules
Under 120 words. No attachments on first contact. No prices. No claims beyond the copy bank. Log the outreach as an activity and the reply, verbatim, as a signal.

## Guardrails
`rules/writing-rules.md` and `rules/do-not-say.md`. Lint and QA before sending.
