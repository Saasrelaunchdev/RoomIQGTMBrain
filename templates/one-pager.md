# Template, one-pager

Audience: [A investors / B operators]. Purpose: [design-partner leave-behind / lender briefing by the investor / community partner brief].

## Structure
- **Header.** What RoomIQ is, in one line from `ops/copy-bank.md`.
- **The problem.** Two sentences, in the audience's language.
- **Three pillars.** One line each, from that audience's value proposition file.
- **How the numbers are built.** Three bullets from `reference/metrics-methodology.md`, verified rows only.
- **What is live today.** SHIPPED capabilities, plainly. PARTIAL ones with their visible part. Nothing else.
- **Proof.** Product facts from the closed set. Customer proof: `[PROOF POINT NEEDED]`.
- **Next step.** One call to action.
- **Footer.** "Estimates are based on observed rents and occupancy, not collected payments. For information, not financial advice."

## Guardrails
Fits one page. One audience. No accuracy, coverage, or price claims. No data-source names. Lint and QA before sending.
