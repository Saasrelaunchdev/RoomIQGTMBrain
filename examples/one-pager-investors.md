## RoomIQ: room-level market data and forecasts for rent-by-the-room investors

**The problem.** A rent-by-the-room deal lives or dies on what each room earns. Most investors still type that number into a spreadsheet from memory or a single listing.

**What RoomIQ does**
- **Find the market.** Up to 3 US cities, ZIPs, or a metro group per dashboard; markets ranked by Market Score; seasonality of occupancy and fill speed.
- **Underwrite the room.** Forecast monthly revenue for any US address from your room mix. Where nearby evidence is thin, the forecast is labeled fallback support with its confidence.
- **Carry it into the deal.** On RoomIQ Pro, the Deal Calculator starts from the forecast and takes your purchase and financing inputs.

**How the numbers are built**
- Forecast = weekly room price x rooms x occupancy x 4.35 weeks, printed on screen.
- Occupancy = occupied rooms divided by total rooms.
- Scenarios are plus and minus 10 percent, labeled "not a calibrated prediction interval".

**Live today:** market dashboard and map, Property Analyzer with room segments and rolling windows, visual comps, Property Predictor forecasts with support and confidence labels, Deal Calculator inputs on RoomIQ Pro.
