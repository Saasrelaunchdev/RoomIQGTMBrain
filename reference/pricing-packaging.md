## Packaging principles to propose to the founder (not decided)
1. Gate decisions, not discovery: free or low tier sees markets and a forecast; Pro gets the Deal Calculator and saved underwrites. Matches the visible Pro badge.
2. Meter what costs money: AI concept generations.
3. Never gate the trust labels. Support, confidence, and formulas show on every tier, because they are the positioning.

## Copy rules until pricing is decided
