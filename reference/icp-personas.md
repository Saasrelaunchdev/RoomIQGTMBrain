# ICP and personas

Load for: targeting, audience choice, sales prep, persona-specific copy. Distills: S2 working notes section 1 (ICP candidates) and the product flow in S1. All personas are WORKING (D-07) until the founder answers G05 and the design-partner program (EXP-001) reports.

## Audience A, acquiring investors (primary)

**Who.** Individual investors and small teams (one to five people) buying or converting single-family homes and townhouses into rent-by-the-room properties, in US Sun Belt metros first.

**Trigger moments.** A house under contract or in diligence. Entering a new metro. Deciding whether to convert a garage. A lender asking for a revenue basis on a DSCR loan.

**Jobs to be done.**
1. Pick a market worth buying in.
2. Check how much coliving supply already sits near an address.
3. Estimate stabilized monthly revenue room by room.
4. Decide the room mix: bathrooms, garage, common space.
5. See whether the deal still works with financing.
6. Keep the underwrite and re-check it later.

**What they use today.** A spreadsheet with typed-in room rents, a marketplace's host calculator, forum threads, a coach's rule of thumb.

**What wins them.** Seeing room-level evidence for their own address, with the uncertainty labeled. The math reproducing on screen.

**What blocks them.** "Where does the data come from?" "How accurate is it?" A first run on an uncovered address that returns a fallback forecast with Low confidence.

## Audience B, operating hosts (secondary)

**Who.** Operators running roughly one to twenty rent-by-the-room houses, often self-managed, who price rooms weekly and fight vacancy.

**Trigger moments.** A room sits empty past its usual fill time. Planning a refresh. Setting the price for a newly added room. Comparing two of their own houses.

**Jobs to be done.**
1. See which rooms earn a premium and why (bath, size).
2. Price a room from rooms that look like it.
3. Watch direction over 7, 30, and 90 days.
4. Decide which upgrade is worth doing.

**What wins them.** A best spread or a visual comp that changes a price this week.

**What blocks them.** Not seeing their own property tracked. Distrust of AI images. Price sensitivity (OPEN, G07).

## Not a GTM audience yet

Lenders, brokers, and acquisition analysts. The product serves them indirectly (DSCR, Evidence Quality), but they need a published backtest before RoomIQ can make the claims they would require. No assets for them until a decision entry changes this.

## Design-partner profile
An Audience A investor with at least one live deal in a covered metro, willing to run it through the Predictor on a call and tell the founder what they decided and why. Ten of them is the first experiment (EXP-001).
