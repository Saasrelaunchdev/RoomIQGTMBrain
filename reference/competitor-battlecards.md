# Competitor battlecards

Load for: sales calls, objection prep, comparison copy. Distills: `reference/competitive-analysis.md`. Three lines per rival: their real strength first, their real gap, how RoomIQ wins. Rival facts dated 2026-11-13; re-verify before external use.

Rule: lead with the rival's genuine strength. A card that pretends a rival is weak loses the room the moment the prospect knows better.

## The status quo: spreadsheet plus forums
- **Strength.** Free, fully flexible, and the investor trusts their own assumptions.
- **Gap.** The room rent and occupancy inputs are guesses; nothing shows how sure the investor should be.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** Run their address live. Show the forecast with the formula, the comps, and the confidence label, then let them compare it to the number in their sheet.

## Platform-native host tools (a marketplace's own dashboard and calculator)
- **Strength.** First-party booking data for that platform's rooms, free to its hosts, and integrated with how they already operate.
- **Gap.** One platform's view, built for hosts already on it, self-reported. An investor evaluating a house they do not own yet is not the primary user.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** Underwrite any US address before the purchase, with room-level evidence and fallback support labeled where nearby evidence is thin. Never claim independence from any platform or name a data source (D-04).

## AirDNA
- **Strength.** Deep short-term rental data and wide recognition as the STR research default.
- **Gap.** Built for nightly stays. Weekly room rents, room occupancy, and fill speed are not its model.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** "AirDNA is built for nightly stays. RoomIQ models weekly room rents and room occupancy." Never "better than AirDNA".

## Mashvisor
- **Strength.** Compares short-term and long-term strategies on the same property, investor-oriented workflow.
- **Gap.** Rent-by-the-room is not one of the strategies it is known for.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** For an investor already set on rent-by-the-room, RoomIQ works at the room level from the first screen.

## Rabbu
- **Strength.** Free short-term rental tools.
- **Gap.** Short-term rental focus.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** Different unit, different decision. Do not compete on price (pricing OPEN).

## Rentometer, HelloData, RentCast
- **Strength.** Address-level rent comps and mature APIs; HelloData tracks concessions in multifamily.
- **Gap.** Whole units. A rent-by-the-room house earns from rooms with different bath and size mixes.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** Room segments and best spread: show that a private-bath room and a shared-bath room in the same house earn differently ($310 vs $238 per week in screen 07).

## DealCheck and similar calculators
- **Strength.** Mature deal math: cash flow, CoC, DSCR, cap rate, multiple strategies.
- **Gap.** Revenue is typed by the user; coliving is not a listed property type.
- **How RoomIQ wins.** The Deal Calculator starts from the RoomIQ forecast instead of a guess (C31, PARTIAL: show inputs prefilled, do not show outputs until captured).

## What to never say about any rival
Rumors, pricing you have not verified this month, "they scrape", "their data is wrong", or any claim about a rival's roadmap.
