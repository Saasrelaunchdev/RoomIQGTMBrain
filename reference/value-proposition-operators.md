# Value proposition, operating hosts

## Brand promise
See how every room in your house earns against rooms like it.

## Positioning for this audience
You price rooms weekly and feel every empty one. RoomIQ shows which of your rooms earn a premium, how similar-looking rooms are priced and how fast they fill, and how your property is trending, so a pricing change rests on evidence.

## Pillar 1, benchmark at the room level
**Headline options:** "Find the room that earns your premium." / "Your house, room by room."
**Reasons to believe (SHIPPED):**
- A Property Signal with a plain-language recommendation for each property (C13).
- Room segments by bathroom (Private, Shared) and size (Cozy, Standard), each with average weekly price and 90-day occupancy (C14).
- The best spread between segments, for example a $72 per week bathroom premium in one Austin capture (C14).
- Rolling 7, 30, and 90-day windows: occupancy, daily price, volatility, and change against the prior window (C15).

## Pillar 2, price from rooms that look like yours
**Headline options:** "Rooms that look like yours, and what they rent for." / "Price against the room, not the zip code."
**Reasons to believe (SHIPPED):**
- Visually Similar Rooms finds rooms whose photos match yours, with each one's weekly price and days to fill, plus the medians (C17).
- Click any match to open that property (C17).

## Pillar 3, plan upgrades with eyes open
**Headline options:** "See an upgrade before you buy the paint." / "Preview the desk before you buy it."
**Reasons to believe:**
- Media Studio generates an AI concept of an upgrade on your listing photo, side by side with the original, from presets such as Add desk, Repaint neutral, and LVP flooring (C18, SHIPPED).
- Every concept is watermarked "AI-GENERATED - NOT ACTUAL PROPERTY CONDITION" (FACT).
- Amenity ROI and per-room rent guidance exist in the Property Predictor (C23, C25, UNVERIFIED: name only).
- No validated upgrade economics yet. Say so.

## What we never promise this audience
Higher rent from an upgrade, faster fill, more bookings, or that their own property is tracked.
