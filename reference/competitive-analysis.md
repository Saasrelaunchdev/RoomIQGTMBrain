## Market shape
There is no established category of room-level investor analytics. Rent-by-the-room investors assemble a view from five alternatives plus the status quo.

| Alternative | Representative tools | Built for | Unit of analysis | Where it stops for this buyer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Status quo | Spreadsheets, forums, coaches | Anyone | Whatever the investor types | No market evidence behind the room rent assumption |
| Platform-native host tools | Marketplace host dashboards, earnings calculators, market reports | That platform's own hosts | Room, inside one platform | One platform's view; self-reported; built for hosts already on it |
| Short-term rental analytics | AirDNA, Mashvisor, Rabbu | Airbnb-style hosts and investors | Night | Nightly stays, not weekly room rents |
| Whole-unit rent comps | Rentometer, HelloData, RentCast | Landlords, multifamily, brokers | Unit | Whole units, not rooms |
| Deal calculators | DealCheck and similar | Buy-and-hold investors | Deal | Revenue is an assumption the user types |
| Data vendors and research | SFR Analytics (room-for-rent analysis), operator reports | Analysts | Varies | Research and data feeds, not an underwriting workflow |

## Scoring vectors (WORKING)
1. **Granularity.** Room, unit, or night.
2. **Evidence transparency.** Does the output show its formula, support, and confidence?
3. **Underwriting depth.** Address to forecast to financed returns in one flow.
4. **Visual evidence.** Photo-based comps.
5. **Coverage independence.** Breadth across sources and markets. OPEN for RoomIQ (G11, G13), so never claimed.

## Where RoomIQ sits (WORKING, unscored)
- Strong on vectors 1 to 4 as SHIPPED capabilities.
- Unknown on vector 5. Do not compete on it.
- Weak where rivals have depth RoomIQ cannot claim: years of history (AirDNA), API ecosystems (Rentometer, HelloData), and the platforms' first-party booking data.

## The wedge
Room-level evidence plus visible uncertainty. Rivals either work at the wrong unit or leave the revenue assumption to the investor. RoomIQ starts from observed room rents and says how far to trust the result.

## Public facts gathered (2026-11-13, re-verify before use)
- A leading marketplace's host tools show each room's price against the metro average and a market insights map; the marketplace publishes monthly market reports and an earnings calculator. Its occupancy data is self-reported from its own bookings.
- AirDNA is a short-term rental data platform, widely cited as the default for STR market research.
- Mashvisor is positioned on comparing short-term and long-term rental strategies on one property.
- Rabbu offers short-term rental tools free and runs a property marketplace.
- Rentometer offers address-level rent estimates and an API built on whole-unit inputs (bedrooms, bathrooms, building type).
- HelloData offers multifamily rent comps and a rent comps API, including concession tracking.
- DealCheck analyzes buy-and-hold, rehab, Airbnb, and vacation rentals; coliving is not listed as a property type.
- SFR Analytics published an analysis of room-for-rent listings against whole-home rent estimates; it is research, not a room-level product.
