## Structure
1. **Title.** The search question or its answer. Under 65 characters.
2. **Opening.** The reader's situation in two sentences. No product.
3. **The method.** How to think about the variable, with a worked example and every unit stated.
4. **Where it goes wrong.** The common mistake, stated plainly.
5. **Doing it with evidence.** One section showing how RoomIQ handles it, SHIPPED capabilities only, one blurred screenshot.
6. **The limit.** What evidence cannot tell you here.
7. **Call to action.** One.

## Guardrails
Teach before showing. Every number carries unit and window. No market statistics published as data (G11, G12). No accuracy, coverage, or price claims. Lint and QA before publishing.
