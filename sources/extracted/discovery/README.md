# RoomIQ GTM Discovery

This folder is the raw input for the RoomIQ go-to-market brief and the CTO-style PRD.

**Status:** collecting screenshots. Synthesis into the GTM brief/PRD is **on hold** until the founder confirms all screenshots are uploaded.

## Files
- `screen-inventory.md`: a screen-by-screen record of layout, word-for-word copy, data values, and observed versus inferred capabilities.
- `working-notes.md`: cross-screen analysis, including the IA map, raw capability list, glossary, data entities, monetization signals, third-party dependencies, QA issues and open questions.
- `feature-classification.md`: the 32 features (scored later on demand and competitiveness) kept separate from the 31 non-feature elements (UI, inputs, trust, account, data sources).
- `coverage-gaps.md`: a click checklist of screens not yet captured (visible-but-unopened controls + typical SaaS screens to verify).
- `screenshots/batch-NN/`: the original screenshots, renamed by screen.

**PRD rule:** the PRD uses only facts visible in the screenshots. Inferred items are kept out of it and listed separately as "to confirm".
