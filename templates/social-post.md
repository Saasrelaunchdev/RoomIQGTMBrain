# Template, social post

Audience: [A / B]. Platform: [LinkedIn / X / community group]. Archetype: [worked example / method / myth check / honest limitation].

## Structure
- **Line 1, the hook.** A number with its unit, a decision, or a candid limit (patterns in `reference/content-engine.md`).
- **Lines 2 to 5, the substance.** One idea. Show the arithmetic if there is any.
- **Line 6, the takeaway.** What the reader should do differently.
- **Optional last line.** One call to action, or none. Communities often reward none.

## Rules
- One idea per post.
- No hashtags stacked; one at most where the platform expects it.
- No emoji.
- Screenshots blurred: host names, listing photos, account details.

## Guardrails
`rules/writing-rules.md` and `rules/do-not-say.md`. No accuracy, coverage, or price claims. Lint and QA before posting.
